## Scope and honesty

US-first. Every page carries a last-reviewed date and a depth badge. Pages that
name specific equipment or prices are flagged internally for shorter review
cycles, because that information rots fastest.

## Corrections

The source is [public on GitHub](https://github.com/jkindrix/wisphandbook).
Every page has an "Edit this page" link and a "Report outdated info" link that
pre-fills an issue. Corrections are genuinely welcome — this is a reference
work, and reference works are maintained or they are dead.

## Disclaimer

This handbook describes the landscape; it is not professional training.
Tower climbing, electrical work, structural attachment, taxes, and law each
have real professionals and real certifications. Where a page touches one of
those, it says so and tells you when to hire one. Nothing here is legal,
financial, or safety advice.

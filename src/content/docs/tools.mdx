Every calculator here also lives inline in the journey, at the page that
teaches the concept behind it — this page just puts them in one place for the
hundredth time you need to run the numbers. All client-side: nothing you type
leaves your browser.

## Break-even

How many customers before the network stops losing money — and how fast each
customer repays their connect cost. Taught in
[Stage 0: Foundations](/journey/foundations/).

## Fresnel zone clearance

How much clear space a radio path needs around the visual line of sight,
using the 60% rule. Taught in
[Stage 1: First Customer](/journey/first-customer/).

<FresnelCalculator />

## Link budget

Whether a planned link delivers your service tier, and with how much margin
to spare for weather and bad days. Taught in
[Stage 1: First Customer](/journey/first-customer/).

## Oversubscription

## What this is

A complete, sequential walkthrough of starting a wireless ISP from ground zero:
what to know, what to learn, what to buy, what to build, and in what order —
from evaluating whether your area can support a WISP at all, through your first
paying customer, to running and growing a real network.

**Scope:** United States–first. Physics and business fundamentals are universal;
regulatory and spectrum specifics are written for the US (FCC) and clearly marked.
